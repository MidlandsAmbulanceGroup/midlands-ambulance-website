Do you charge for travel to Staffordshire?

Any travel costs are shown in your quote, so you see the full price before you book.

Yes. Every booking includes a risk-assessed medical plan, with the local hospitals and NHS ambulance arrangements for your venue.

What happens if someone needs hospital?

We treat on site. When someone needs hospital, we call NHS ambulance services and hand over with a full digital patient record.
