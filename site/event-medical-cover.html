It depends on attendance, the activity, the venue and how far the nearest hospital is. Our medical cover guide below gives an indication; your actual cover is confirmed in your medical plan, using the Purple Guide approach.

What happens if someone needs hospital?

Our crew treats the patient on site, calls NHS ambulance services when needed and hands over with a full record of care.

Do you cover events outside the Midlands?

Yes. We're based in Swadlincote, Derbyshire and cover events across the UK, and abroad in Paris.
