What's included

Everything your event needs on the medical side

Every booking starts with a risk assessment. We then send the right mix of clinicians and vehicles, and stay until the site is clear.

  • Medical plan and resource assessmentWritten for your event and ready for your Safety Advisory Group.
  • Clinicians matched to your riskFrom first aiders to paramedics, nurses and doctors.
  • Dual Crewed Ambulances and Fast Response VehiclesA treatment base on site and rapid reach across large areas.
  • Treatment area and equipmentDefibrillators, oxygen, trauma and medical kits on every deployment.
  • Radio contact with your controlWe work alongside your security, stewards and event control.
  • Post-event reportA summary of every patient contact for your records.

Questions organisers ask

Planning your cover

Not sure what you need? Call 0333 335 8999 and we'll talk it through.

How many medics do I need?

It depends on attendance, the activity, the venue and how far the nearest hospital is. We work it out in your medical plan using the Purple Guide approach.

How far ahead should I book?

[Recommended notice period, for example 6 to 8 weeks for large events and peak summer weekends.]

What happens if someone needs hospital?

Our crew treats the patient on site, calls NHS ambulance services when needed and hands over with a full record of care.

Do you cover events outside the Midlands?

Yes. We're based in Swadlincote, Derbyshire and cover events across the UK on request.
