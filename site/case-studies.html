Case studies

Events we have covered

How we planned and delivered medical cover for organisers across the UK.

Triathlon · Oxfordshire

Banbury Triathlon

The brief: Medical cover for a triathlon lasting several hours, with risks spread across the swim exit, transition, a road cycle course and the finish line. These range from cramp and dehydration to collisions on the bike leg and cardiac emergencies.

What we provided: Clinicians at the swim exit, transition and finish, with mobile cover along the course, all set out in a written medical plan.

The outcome: Rebooked for a third year running. The organiser, Sarah Shepherd, said: “If I could give more than five stars, I would – Midlands Ambulance Group saved my event!”

Equestrian · Leicestershire

Ministry of Defence Equestrian Trials

The brief: Medical cover for the MoD's Equestrian Trials at the Defence Animal Training Regiment, Melton Mowbray, where falls, kicks and collisions between riders, horses and obstacles can happen suddenly.

What we provided: A medical team working within MoD protocols and standards, alongside MoD personnel and the event organisers.

The outcome: Renewed for a third year running.

Charity · Birmingham

Charity sleep out, Birmingham city centre

The brief: Overnight cover in October 2026 for people sleeping outdoors in the city centre to raise funds and awareness to end homelessness, with cold weather the main risk.

What we provided: Frontline ambulance clinicians and a response car on site through the night.

The outcome: The event raised funds and awareness, and we look forward to working with the organisers again.
