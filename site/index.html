Before you book

Can't see your question? Call 0333 335 8999.

Do you cover events outside the Midlands?

Yes. We are based in Swadlincote, Derbyshire and cover events across the UK, including Bournemouth and Glasgow, and abroad in Paris. Travel, accommodation and crew rest are planned into your quote.

How many medics will my event need?

It depends on attendance, the activity, the audience, the site and how far the nearest hospital is. Try our medical cover guide for an indication. Your actual cover is discussed and confirmed in your event medical plan.

Do you write the event medical plan?

Yes. Every booking includes a medical plan and risk assessment, written to support your Safety Advisory Group and licensing conditions.

Which vehicles can you provide?

Dual Crewed Ambulances and Fast Response Vehicles, each crewed by our own clinicians.
