Our story

Built on real emergency experience

We started covering events in 2019 and began teaching first aid in 2020. Today our team covers festivals, sport, film sets and community events across the Midlands and beyond, from our base in Swadlincote, Derbyshire.

I started Midlands Ambulance Group because I wanted to build something different: a medical provider that understands real emergencies, not just first aid scenarios. My background is in frontline ambulance work, and it taught me that effective event medical cover is about more than having a team and a first aid kit on site. It is about clinical judgement, preparedness, strong leadership and the ability to respond when the unexpected happens.

I am proud of the time, commitment and determination that have gone into building MAG from the ground up, developing the right relationships and creating a business built on professionalism, integrity and patient-centred care. What sets us apart is that we bring real-world emergency care experience into the events industry. We combine appropriately skilled clinicians, robust planning and a collaborative approach to deliver medical provision tailored to each event's own risks.

My ambition is for Midlands Ambulance Group to become a trusted, nationally recognised provider operating throughout the UK. I want us to be known not simply for turning up, but for giving clients confidence that their medical provision has been carefully considered, professionally delivered and built around the safety of the people attending their events. For me, MAG is about raising expectations of what event medical cover should be, one event, one partnership and one patient at a time.

John BlakeManaging Director, Midlands Ambulance Group

How we work

The same standards at every event

01

Planned in writing

Every booking has a risk-assessed medical plan, built the way the Purple Guide describes, ready for your Safety Advisory Group, council and insurers.

02

Led on the day

An experienced event lead briefs the crew before gates open. Our event leads are trained in major incident management (MIMMS), and our clinicians use Ten Second Triage, the national triage standard.

03

Recorded and reported

Every patient contact goes on a digital patient record, and you receive a post-event report. Clinical governance is led by our Paramedic Clinical Lead and Medical Director.
