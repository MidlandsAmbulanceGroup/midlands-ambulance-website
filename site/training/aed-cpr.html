AED and CPR

A short, hands-on session for anyone who may need to use a defibrillator, ideal for clubs and workplaces that have just installed one.

Book a place →Book for your team

What you'll learn

Skills you can use straight away

  • Recognising cardiac arrestSpotting the signs and calling 999 quickly.
  • Effective CPRRate, depth and swapping rescuers.
  • Using a defibrillatorPad placement and following the prompts.
  • After the shockThe recovery position and handing over to paramedics.
