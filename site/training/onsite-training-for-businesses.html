Who it suits

Training built around your workplace

We use scenarios that match your setting, so your team practises on the kind of emergency they could actually face.

  • Offices and businessesCPR, defibrillator use and common workplace emergencies.
  • Sports clubs and gymsCollapse, head injuries and using the club defibrillator.
  • Schools, nurseries and youth groupsPaediatric first aid for staff and volunteers.
  • Care and healthcare teamsBasic and Intermediate Life Support for clinical staff.
  • Community groups and charitiesPractical first aid for volunteers who run events.

Questions

Before you book

What do we need to provide?

A room big enough for everyone to kneel on the floor, and a table. We bring the manikins, training defibrillators and dressings.

Can you train evening or weekend shifts?

Yes. We can run sessions at times that suit your rota.

Can you tailor the content?

Yes. Tell us about your workplace and any incidents you have had, and we will build them into the scenarios.

Is this a First Aid at Work qualification?

No. Our courses aren't regulated qualifications. If you need First Aid at Work or Emergency First Aid at Work, tell us and we'll talk through your options.
