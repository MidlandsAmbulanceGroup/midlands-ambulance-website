Questions

Before you book

Can you run this at our workplace?

Yes. We bring the manikins, defibrillator and kit to your site anywhere in the Midlands.

Does this meet Ofsted EYFS requirements?

No. This course isn't the regulated Paediatric First Aid qualification required in Early Years settings. Talk to us if you need that.

Can you run it for a parent group or nursery staff?

Yes, at your venue. Tell us how many people and we will plan the session around your group.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes, a certificate of attendance. Our courses aren't regulated qualifications, so check what your employer or insurer needs.
