Questions

Before you book

Can you run this at our workplace?

Yes. We bring the manikins, defibrillator and kit to your site anywhere in the Midlands.

Does this meet First Aid at Work rules?

This course isn't a regulated Emergency First Aid at Work or First Aid at Work qualification. If your workplace needs one of those, tell us and we'll talk through your options.

Do I need any experience?

No. The course starts from the basics and most of the time is spent practising.
