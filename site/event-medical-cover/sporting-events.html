What we plan for

The risks at sporting events

Every plan starts with the risks particular to your event. These are the ones we see most often.

  • Impact and collision injuriesFast assessment on the field of play, including head injuries.
  • Cardiac eventsDefibrillators and clinicians who can lead a resuscitation.
  • Long coursesResponse vehicles and spaced teams so no part of the route is far from help.
  • Spectators as well as athletesA plan that covers the crowd, not just the competitors.
