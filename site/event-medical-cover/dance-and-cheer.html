The risks at dance and cheer competitions

Every plan starts with the risks particular to your event. These are the ones we see most often.

  • Falls from stunts and liftsRapid assessment of neck, back and head injuries.
  • Sprains and fracturesSplinting, strapping and clear advice for parents.
  • Fainting and exhaustionLong days in warm venues, with young performers.
  • Large, busy venuesSpectator cover as well as the competition floor.
