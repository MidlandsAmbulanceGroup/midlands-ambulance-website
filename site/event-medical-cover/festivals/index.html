What we plan for

The risks at festivals and concerts

Every plan starts with the risks particular to your event. These are the ones we see most often.

  • Crowd density at stages and barriersClinicians positioned where pressure builds, with clear routes out.
  • Heat, dehydration and cold nightsWater, shade and welfare plans built into the medical plan.
  • Alcohol and drug-related illnessExperienced teams who recognise and manage intoxication safely.
  • Overnight and camping coverShift patterns that keep the site covered around the clock.

Questions

Before you book

How many medics does a festival need?

It depends on attendance, the type of music and audience, the site and how far the nearest hospital is. We set the numbers in your medical plan.

Can you work with our welfare provider?

Yes. We agree handovers and radio channels with welfare, security and event control before the gates open.
