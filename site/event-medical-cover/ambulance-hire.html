Questions

Choosing a vehicle

Which vehicle does my event need?

It depends on the size of the site, the crowd and the activities. Your medical plan sets out which vehicles you need and where they go. Many events use one of each.

Do your vehicles come with a crew?

Always. Every vehicle is crewed by our own clinicians and run under the same medical plan as the rest of your team.

Can a vehicle act as the first aid point?

Yes. A branded vehicle is easy for guests to find, and a Dual Crewed Ambulance gives patients somewhere warm and private to be treated.

Can you bring vehicles to events outside the Midlands?

Yes. Our vehicles have worked in London, Wales and on the south coast, and travel is planned into your quote.

How we work

The same standards at every event

01

Planned in writing

Every booking has a risk-assessed medical plan, built the way the Purple Guide describes, ready for your Safety Advisory Group, council and insurers.

02

Led on the day

An experienced event lead briefs the crew before gates open. Our event leads are trained in major incident management (MIMMS), and our clinicians use Ten Second Triage, the national triage standard.

03

Recorded and reported

Every patient contact goes on a digital patient record, and you receive a post-event report. Clinical governance is led by our Paramedic Clinical Lead and Medical Director.
