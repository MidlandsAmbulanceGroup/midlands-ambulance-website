Dual Crewed Ambulance

A treatment base on wheels

A two-person crew with the space and equipment to assess and treat patients privately on site.

  • Two clinicians
  • Defibrillator, oxygen and suction
  • Trauma, immobilisation and medical kits
  • Somewhere warm and private to treat patients
Fast Response Vehicle

Speed across large sites

A single clinician in a fully equipped car, ideal for long courses, showgrounds and spread-out festival sites.

  • One clinician, fully equipped
  • Quick to reach patients on roads and tracks
  • A visible, branded first aid point
  • Works alongside a DCA or on its own

If a patient needs hospital

We treat on site and hand over to the NHS

Our crews assess and treat on site. When someone needs hospital, we call NHS ambulance services and hand over with a full record of care.
