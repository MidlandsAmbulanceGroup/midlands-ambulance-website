Community

First aid cover for a village fete or school fair

A practical checklist for community organisers planning their first event.

Most community events are low risk, but a little planning makes a big difference when someone does get hurt.

A simple checklist

  • Book cover early, especially for summer weekends.
  • Agree where the first aid point will be and sign it clearly.
  • Tell stewards and volunteers how to call for help.
  • Plan for hot weather, with shade and water.
  • Check whether your council wants a medical plan.

How much will it cost?

It depends on the length of the day and the cover needed. Ask about our community and charity rates.

On the day

  • Show the medical team round the site when they arrive.
  • Give them a radio or a mobile number for the event organiser.
  • Make sure stewards know where the first aid point is.
  • Keep a vehicle route clear in case an ambulance is needed.

Have a defibrillator to hand

Find out where the nearest public access defibrillator is, or ask your medical team to bring one. Early CPR and defibrillation give the best chance of survival from cardiac arrest.
