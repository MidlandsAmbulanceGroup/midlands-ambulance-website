Planning

What goes in an event medical plan?

The sections a Safety Advisory Group expects to see, in plain English.

A medical plan explains how injured or unwell people at your event will be found, treated and, if needed, handed over to the NHS.

What it usually includes

  • Event details, timings and expected attendance.
  • The risk assessment behind your medical resources.
  • Staff numbers, grades and positions.
  • Vehicles, equipment and the treatment area.
  • Communication with event control.
  • Arrangements with local NHS services and hospitals.
  • Major incident procedures.

Who writes it

We write the plan for every booking, so you can share it with your Safety Advisory Group and insurers.
