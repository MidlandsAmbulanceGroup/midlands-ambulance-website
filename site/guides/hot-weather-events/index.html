Keeping people safe at events in hot weather

How to spot heat illness and what to put in place before a heatwave weekend.

Heat illness is one of the most common reasons people need medical help at summer events, and much of it can be prevented.

What to put in place

  • Free drinking water and plenty of shade.
  • Announcements reminding people to drink and cover up.
  • Extra medical cover when temperatures are forecast to be high.

Signs to watch for

Headache, dizziness, cramps, confusion and hot, dry skin. Anyone who is confused or has stopped sweating needs urgent medical help.
