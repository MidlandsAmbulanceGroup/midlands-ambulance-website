Clinical

Medical grades explained, from first aider to paramedic

What each clinical grade can do, and when you need them at your event.

Event medical providers use a range of clinical grades. Knowing the difference helps you understand a quote and compare like with like.

First aider

Trained to treat minor injuries and illness and to recognise when someone needs more help.

Emergency Medical Technician

Assesses and treats a wide range of injuries and medical emergencies, often working alongside paramedics.

Paramedic

A registered clinician with advanced assessment skills and a wider range of medicines, able to lead care for serious illness and trauma.

Nurses and doctors

Used at larger and higher-risk events to run treatment areas and provide senior clinical leadership.
