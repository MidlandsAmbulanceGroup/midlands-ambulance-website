How many first aiders do I need at my event?

The factors that decide the size of your medical team, and why there is no single number.

There is no fixed ratio that works for every event. The right number depends on who is coming, what they will be doing and how quickly help can reach them.

What decides the numbers

  • Attendance, including peak numbers on site at once.
  • The activity. A motorsport day carries different risks to a craft fair.
  • The audience. Age, alcohol and the likely mood of the crowd.
  • The site. Size, terrain, stages and how spread out it is.
  • Duration and weather. Long, hot or overnight events need more.
  • Distance to hospital and how busy local NHS services are.

How organisers usually work it out

Most organisers use a risk-based approach set out in the Purple Guide, the industry guidance for outdoor events. It scores the event and suggests the level of cover, which is then adjusted for local factors.

Getting it right

Your Safety Advisory Group may ask to see how you reached your numbers. We write that reasoning into your medical plan, so you have something clear to show them.
